SendIt itself is GPL-3.0-licensed (see [LICENSE](LICENSE)).

**Meshy assets.** The rows that say "Generated with Meshy" are AI-generated models made on the owner's paid Meshy Pro plan, whose terms give the customer ownership of the output (they are not CC0 or Creative Commons). Every one was cleaned up by hand-written scripts in `tools/meshy` and checked for real logos and lettering, and the owner releases them under this repository's GPL-3.0. Raw Meshy downloads are not in the repository.

**Synthesized audio.** Since 0.3.1 most of the sound is made by SendIt's own scripts in `tools/audio/` (engine, road, crash, traffic, score, menu and police sets), with no recorded input. The owner dedicates those generated files to the public domain under CC0; the scripts that make them are GPL-3.0 like the rest of the code. The recorded sounds that remain (pass-bys, tyre squeal, road roar, wind) are third-party CC0 recordings, credited below.

Code: SendIt original. Driving-feel ideas informed by KenneyNL/Starter-Kit-Racing (MIT) and godot-demo-projects truck_town (MIT); no code copied verbatim.
